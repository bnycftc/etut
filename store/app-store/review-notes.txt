Etüt is a study timer, weekly study plan and mock-exam tracker for students preparing for YKS in Turkey. No account or sign-in is needed; all data stays on the device and the app makes no network requests.
1. On first launch choose YKS, then Sayısal, tap İleri, tap İleri again, then pick any birth year.
2. On that last step tap "Örnek verilerle gez" (Explore with sample data). The app opens with a few weeks of sample sessions, mock exams and topic marks.
3. Sayaç tab: the "Bugünün planı" (Today's plan) card lists today's study blocks. Tap "Başlat" on a block; the timer starts on that subject and topic.
4. While the timer runs, lock the iPhone: the session is shown as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
5. Plan tab: this week's plan day by day, and the study minutes available on each weekday (adjustable).
6. Denemeler tab: the net chart. Tap a mock exam, then its analysis button ("Analizi düzenle") to see wrong and blank answers by topic.
7. Sayaç tab > Konular: the topic list with progress. Tap a topic to open its detail (time studied, last session, wrong and blank answers, Başlat).
8. Denemeler tab > "Net hesapla": type correct and wrong counts; the net of each section and the total are calculated.
9. Ayarlar > "Örnek moddan çık" leaves the sample data and returns to the student's own (empty) data.
The app is no longer iPhone-only: iPad is supported. The Live Activity is shown on iPhone.
