Etüt is an on-device study timer and mock-exam tracker for Turkish exam students (YKS, LGS, KPSS). It has no server, no account, no groups and makes no network requests; no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking. No data is collected by the developer (App Privacy: Data Not Collected).

There is nothing to sign in to: on first launch choose a birth year and an exam, then use the app. Users under 15 get the same on-device app without the study-stats image sharing.

All data stays in the app's local database on the device. Data leaves the device only when the user taps export (backup file, CSV or a study-stats image) and picks a destination in the system share sheet; nothing is uploaded by the app.

Permissions: add-only Photos access, asked only when the user saves the study-stats image. Notification permission is asked only if the user turns on a local reminder (Ayarlar > Hatırlatıcılar); reminders are scheduled on the device, there is no push. The Live Activity and the Home Screen widget show data the app writes to its own App Group on the device.

Ayarlar > Hakkında > İletişim opens the Mail app with the app and OS version in the subject; the app itself sends nothing.

Privacy policy, terms and licenses: Ayarlar (Settings) > Hakkında.

Etüt is not an official app and is not affiliated with ÖSYM or MEB. Exam dates shown in the app are estimates the user can change.
