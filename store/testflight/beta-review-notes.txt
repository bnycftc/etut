Closed beta of Etüt 0.5.0 for adults (18+) only: the server refuses accounts of users aged 15-17 during this beta. Etüt is a study timer and mock-exam tracker for Turkish exam students; this build adds optional, invite-only study groups (at most 30 people). There is no chat, no direct messages, no feed, no public profile, no group search or listing and no user search. The only free text is the nickname and the group name, both filtered on the server. Members see who is studying now, daily and weekly study time and an in-group ranking, and can send fixed ready-made reactions. No ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking; no push notifications.

No sign-in is needed: the group account is anonymous (no e-mail, phone or password). Demo: on first launch choose birth year 2000 and the exam YKS. Open the Gruplar tab, create the group account with any nickname, then under "Koda katıl" enter the invite code [DEMO KODU] and tap "Katılma isteği gönder". The demo group approves join requests automatically, so it appears in the list at once; its other members are demo accounts. In real groups the founder approves each request.

Safety: tap a member in the group to report ("Bildir") or block ("Engelle"); founders can also report or block a join request. Parents can link from their own device (Ayarlar > Veli modu) to lock group settings and set a daily group time limit. Account deletion: Gruplar > Grup hesabı > Grup hesabımı sil, or Ayarlar > Veriler > Tüm verileri sil.

The birth year never leaves the device; users under 15 get the on-device app with no account, no groups and no network requests. Privacy policy and terms: Ayarlar > Hakkında.
